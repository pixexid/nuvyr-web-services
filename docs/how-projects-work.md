# How our projects work

[Repository home](../README.md) · [Services](services.md) · [Service area](service-area.md) · [How projects work](how-projects-work.md) · [Architecture](architecture.md)

We start with the business problem and agree on the work before making changes. The exact process depends on the service and written scope; the live service pages are the source of truth for current offer details.

## 1. Inquiry

[Contact us](https://nuvyr.com/contact) with your business name, website, services, service area, and what you want to change or understand. Choose a service or tell us you are not sure. Please do not send passwords, API keys, payment details, customer lists, or other sensitive records.

## 2. Scope

We discuss the problem and fit, then agree the relevant deliverables, responsibilities, milestones, review points, and terms in writing before work begins. Access, if needed, is arranged securely after agreement. If the work is not a fit, we say so.

## 3. Build and review

We carry out the agreed work and involve you at the review points in the scope. You confirm business facts and provide feedback. For a Snapshot, this stage is a documented diagnostic and analysis; implementation is a separate, optional engagement. For website work, checks and launch follow the agreed plan.

## 4. Handoff

We share the agreed deliverables and explain what was done, what was checked, and any remaining next steps. Any ongoing work is separately scoped. A handoff does not imply a guaranteed search, AI, or business outcome.

## What this overview does not promise

We do not promise a fixed price or timeline here, a particular result, or that every inquiry is a fit. The live pages and the written agreement govern the actual service and project scope.
