# Our services

[Repository home](../README.md) · [Services](services.md) · [Service area](service-area.md) · [How projects work](how-projects-work.md) · [Architecture](architecture.md)

We offer four service families for small service businesses. The live pages are the source of truth for current scope, availability, terms, and pricing. We agree the work in writing before it begins; we do not publish prices here.

## Service comparison

| Service | Starting point | What we work on |
|---|---|---|
| Websites | A site no longer explains the business or makes the next step clear | A scoped refresh of brand, pages, copy, and the customer journey |
| Search visibility | Business information and search foundations need attention | Agreed fixes and, where suitable, ongoing work to keep information accurate |
| Automation | Repetitive work needs a practical improvement | A scoped workflow with suitable checks and human oversight |
| AI Visibility Snapshot | A business wants a documented view of selected AI responses | A pilot diagnostic, foundation review, and prioritized recommendations |

## Websites

[Website Refresh](https://nuvyr.com/transformations) brings visual direction, a focused set of English pages, verified service copy, and an inquiry path into an agreed project. We assess what can stay before recommending replacement. Scope, milestones, revisions, and handoff are agreed before work begins.

[Website Updates](https://nuvyr.com/website-updates) is a separate option when an existing site needs scoped fixes, updates, or optional ongoing care rather than a redesign.

## Search visibility

[Search visibility](https://nuvyr.com/local-growth-systems) starts with a bounded foundation: accurate business facts, useful and indexable pages, eligible profiles, and clear contact paths. Ongoing work can be scoped to the business. We do not promise rankings or customer outcomes.

## Automation

[Automation](https://nuvyr.com/ai-integrations) focuses on practical workflows that can reduce repetitive manual work. We agree the task and its limits, including where a person reviews or approves an action. The live page describes the current offer.

## AI Visibility Snapshot

The [AI Visibility Snapshot](https://nuvyr.com/ai-visibility-audit) is an optional pilot diagnostic. We record a bounded set of prompts and conditions, review responses and sources, and deliver observations and prioritized recommendations. It is not a universal ranking or a promise of future mentions. Implementation is separate and optional.
