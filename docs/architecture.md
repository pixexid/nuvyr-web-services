# Our public-safe architecture and methodology

[Repository home](../README.md) · [Services](services.md) · [Service area](service-area.md) · [How projects work](how-projects-work.md) · [Architecture](architecture.md)

We use technology to make our services understandable and the next step usable. The production application is private; this overview describes public-facing principles, not private code or configuration. The live site is the source of truth for current content and offers.

## Website system

```text
Readable service and project pages
→ clear navigation and contact path
→ inquiry reviewed by a person
→ agreed project scope and work
```

The nuvyr.com site uses TanStack Start on Cloudflare Workers. Technology can change; the purpose is to deliver useful pages and a clear inquiry path.

## Search and discovery

We make important content readable as HTML, use clear page structure and canonical URLs, and align structured data with visible facts. Our search approach emphasizes accurate business information, relevant service pages, and usable contact paths. These foundations do not guarantee rankings or AI mentions.

## Accessibility and privacy

We aim for accessible navigation, readable content, keyboard use, visible focus, responsive layouts, and respect for reduced-motion preferences. We ask people not to submit credentials, payment details, customer lists, or sensitive records in an inquiry. We use inquiry information to respond and scope the request, not for advertising sales.

## Public methodology

Our [methodology](https://nuvyr.com/methodology) explains the AI Visibility Snapshot and its limits. We prepare a bounded set of relevant prompts, record the provider surface and conditions for each observation, and capture responses and sources. We distinguish mentions, citations, factual accuracy, and response order; order in a response is not a universal rank.

We review the website and other relevant public sources alongside the observations, then separate verified facts, observations, inferences, recommendations, and limitations. We check evidence and calculations before delivery and identify what could not be tested. Results describe the tested conditions at a point in time; they do not prove universal visibility, future mentions, customer exposure, or conversion. Illustrative examples are labeled as samples, not customer results.

## The customer journey

Our broader approach organizes work into four stages:

1. **Found:** make accurate information and pages available for discovery.
2. **Trusted:** show real, verifiable evidence and a consistent identity.
3. **Contacted:** make the next action and contact paths clear and usable.
4. **Followed up:** support a practical response process with appropriate human judgment.

## Public/private boundary

We can discuss the public website, service model, high-level technology, and published methodology. We do not publish private source code, credentials, provider configuration, customer records, or private operational procedures. We do not present a demonstration as a client result.
